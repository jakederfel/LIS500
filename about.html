About Us

Adi Govil

My name is Adi Govil, and I am an international senior at UW–Madison studying data science and information science. My parents are ethnically Indian, but I grew up in Indonesia before coming to the United States for university.

Outside of school, I enjoy spending time with friends, watching movies, travelling, and trying new types of food. Growing up in Indonesia and then studying in the United States has given me different perspectives on the places and communities I am part of.

One of the main reasons I joined LIS 500 is that I want to apply what I learn in my future career as a data analyst. I want to understand how decisions involving data can affect people and how I can approach that responsibility more thoughtfully.

Jake Derfel

I’m Jake Derfel, and I am a senior at UW–Madison studying data science. I am originally from Chicago and plan to move back to the city to work full time after graduation.

In my free time, I love playing tennis and pickleball, going on runs, listening to music, and hanging out with friends and family. A fun fact about me is that I am an extra in a small Chicago-based movie.

I joined LIS 500 because I want to gain a more complete understanding of code and power before I graduate. As someone studying data science, I want to think about both how technology works and how it affects the people who use it.

What connects our interests?

We both study data science and expect technology to be part of our future careers. This class gives us a reason to pause and ask questions that a technical assignment might not always bring up. Who is represented in a dataset? Who gets to make design decisions? How could a system treat people differently even when its creators intend to be fair?

Our goal for this project is to make those questions approachable for other students who are starting to explore them.
