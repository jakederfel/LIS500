What happens to my camera video?

The machine model processes frames in your browser. Our page does not record or upload your camera footage. It downloads the model and its JavaScript libraries from external hosts. Stop camera turns off the camera stream used by this page.

What does the model recognize?

  • Mugs: The class trained with our mug images.
  • Bottles: The class trained with our bottle images.
  • Empty: The class for scenes without either target object.

Our model chooses between these three labels. An unfamiliar object, or both objects appearing together, can still receive one of these labels. Empty is a training category, not proof that the model understands every object that is absent from the picture.

Our project

We trained this image classifier using Google Teachable Machine and connected the exported model to our LIS 500 website. The webpage adapts the Coding Train example to show a live camera image and its predicted label.

Our experiment asks how the examples we select influence what a model recognizes. We want to examine whether its predictions change when the background, lighting, or position of an object changes. This connects to our course discussions about the human choices behind automated systems.

Dataset and group contributions

We collected 240 original training images. Adi contributed 40 mug images, 40 bottle images, and 40 empty-scene images. Jake contributed the same amounts. Each member contributed 120 images, giving us 80 images per class.
