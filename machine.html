What Can Our Machine Recognize?

Our model was trained to recognize three categories:

  • Mugs: Images containing a mug.
  • Bottles: Images containing a bottle.
  • Empty: Images without a mug or bottle.

What are its limitations?

Our model can only choose between these three labels. If you show it an unfamiliar object or both objects at the same time, it will still choose one of these labels. The Empty category does not mean the machine understands every other object.

How Our Model Works

We trained this image classifier using Google Teachable Machine and connected the exported model to our LIS 500 website. The webpage adapts the Coding Train example to show a live camera image and its predicted label.

What are we trying to learn?

Our experiment looks at how the images we select influence what a machine recognizes. We want to see if its predictions change when the background, lighting, or position of an object changes.

This connects to our course discussions about the human choices behind automated systems and how those choices can influence their results.
