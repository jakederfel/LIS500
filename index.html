Hi! Welcome to our Project!

Technology is part of our everyday lives. But who builds it, whose experiences shape it, and who gets left out?

We’re Adi Govil and Jake Derfel, two students at UW–Madison. This website brings together what we are learning in LIS 500 about technology, identity, and inequality. We want to think more carefully about the choices behind the tools we use and the tools we might build in the future.
